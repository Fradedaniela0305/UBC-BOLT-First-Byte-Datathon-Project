# VCFC Data Strategy Analysis
**UBC BOLT Datathon**

## Overview

This project presents a data-driven strategy analysis conducted for Vancouver City FC (VCFC) as part of the **UBC BOLT Datathon**. The objective was to analyze fan behavior, revenue streams, and operational data to uncover insights that could inform marketing strategy, improve retention, and identify new revenue opportunities.

The analysis combines data cleaning, statistical testing, and visualization to translate raw data into actionable business recommendations.

---

## Tools & Technologies

- Python  
- R  
- tidyverse  
- ggplot2  

---

## Data Sources

- Fan demographic data  
- Merchandise sales data  
- Stadium operations and attendance data  

---

## Methods

- Data cleaning and preprocessing in Python and R  
- Exploratory data analysis (EDA) across demographic and transactional data  
- Spearman correlation statistical testing

- Visualization techniques:
  - Heatmaps
  - Time-series analysis
  - Product category breakdowns

---

## Key Insights

- Identified demographic segments most strongly associated with merchandise spending  
- Uncovered misalignment between marketing efforts and high-value fan groups  
- Detected inefficiencies in loyalty incentives and seasonal pass retention  
- Highlighted off-season opportunities to stabilize revenue  

---

## Recommendations

- Optimize seasonal pass pricing and incentives for high-retention demographics  
- Refine merchandise marketing strategies by product category and fan profile  
- Introduce targeted off-season campaigns to maintain fan engagement and revenue  
- Align marketing channels more closely with observed fan behavior patterns  

---

## Outcome

The project delivered data-backed strategic recommendations aimed at improving:
- Seasonal pass retention  
- Merchandise revenue  
- Marketing efficiency  
- Long-term fan engagement
